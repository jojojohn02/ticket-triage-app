How Signal reads a ticket

01
It listens for tone. Words like "urgent" or "unacceptable" mark a frustrated customer. Words like "confused" or "not sure" mark someone who's just unsure. No tone words, no strong feeling — that's neutral.
02
It listens for topic. "Password" or "locked out" points to a login problem. "Charge" or "refund" points to billing. "Error" or "down" points to a technical bug.
03
It listens for impact. Phrases like "can't access" or "site is down" signal something serious. Phrases like "would be nice" or "just a suggestion" signal it can wait.
04
It combines all three into one priority — the same rulebook you'd hand a new agent on day one. Nothing hidden, nothing you can't double-check yourself.

This is a working demo of the "brain" from the project plan — the part that reads tone, topic, and impact. It isn't yet connected to a real help desk like Zendesk, and it isn't learning from your team's own tickets. Both are very doable — they're just the next stage, not this one.
